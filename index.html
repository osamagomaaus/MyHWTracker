A little more organized

Everything you need to keep moving.

Simple tools turn a long list of schoolwork into a plan you can actually follow.

▤

Assignments in one place

Add assignments yourself or import coursework from Google Classroom. Search and filter your list when you need to find something fast.

▦

Dates you can see

Use the calendar and planner to look ahead, spot upcoming deadlines, and make time for each task.

✓

Progress that feels good

Set priorities, estimate how long work will take, mark assignments complete, and see your progress add up.

◷

Helpful reminders

Turn on in-app reminders for assignments due today or already overdue. You choose whether to enable them.

Getting started

From scattered to sorted.

Homework Tracker is a Windows desktop app. Create an account to use the app, then decide whether you want to connect Google Classroom.

See how your information is handled →
  1. 01

    Sign in

    Create or sign in to your Homework Tracker account. Email verification is required.

  2. 02

    Make it yours

    Add your assignments manually, or connect Google Classroom and choose coursework to import.

  3. 03

    Keep moving

    Check your dashboard, calendar, and planner as you work through your assignments.
